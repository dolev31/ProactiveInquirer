Answer the task by reasoning one sentence at a time. Write the NEXT sentence of the
reasoning chain and nothing else: no preamble, no list, no repetition of the sentences you
have already written. Each sentence you write is also used verbatim as the query that
retrieves the next paragraphs, so state the entity you need information about explicitly
rather than referring to it as it, they or the former, and put the part you most need
evidence for in the sentence rather than leaving it implied.

Base each sentence on the paragraphs below. If they do not yet contain what the next step of
the reasoning requires, write the sentence that states what that step needs, naming the
entity involved, so that the retrieval it triggers can supply it. Do not invent a fact that
no paragraph supports and do not restate a paragraph verbatim; carry the reasoning forward
by one step.

When the paragraphs are sufficient to conclude, write a final sentence that begins with
"So the answer is:" followed by the answer, and stop there. A chain that concludes early on
sufficient evidence is better than one padded with restatements of what is already known.
